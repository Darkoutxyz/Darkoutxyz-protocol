# Darkout Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

**Vision.** Every household owns a small, free toolkit that keeps working when power and network do not.

**Mission.** Build that toolkit in the open, fund it with a community coin, and publish every claim with its proof. Darkout is a memecoin. It carries no promise of returns, and nothing in this document is financial advice.

## Roadmap

**Phase 1: Pocket web kit.** Assistant, street atlas, triage cards, light and distress beam, and a radio receiver helper, running in a phone or laptop browser.

**Phase 2: Native handset app.** Short-range relay of text and voice from handset to handset, including in the background.

**Phase 3: Long-range boxes.** Small radio units that pair with the app and carry messages across a city.

**Phase 4: Relief wallet.** A public wallet that funds kits and radios where they are needed, with every spend visible on-chain.

Phases are intentions, ordered by cost and usefulness. A later phase starts only when the earlier ones prove out.
